Cancellation Policy

Effective 7 August 2026 · Last updated 13 August 2026

You can cancel an order free of charge at any time before it is despatched. Because orders are paid on delivery, cancelling before despatch costs you nothing.

How to cancel

  1. Open Profile → My Orders in the Soho app.
  2. Open the order you want to cancel.
  3. Use the cancel option if it is shown, or contact support with your order number.

The fastest route is WhatsApp — see the support page. Orders move to the courier quickly, so tell us as soon as you know.

When you can cancel

Order status Can you cancel?
Pending or confirmed Yes, free of charge
Being packed Usually yes — contact support immediately
Despatched or out for delivery No. Refuse the parcel at the door, or return it after delivery
Delivered No — use the return process instead

Cancelling part of an order

You can cancel individual items before despatch. The delivery charge is set by your delivery address — ৳80 inside Dhaka, ৳150 elsewhere in Bangladesh — and not by what the order is worth, so removing an item does not change it. You simply stop paying for the item you removed.

Refusing at the door

If the parcel has already been despatched, you can decline it when the courier arrives. You pay nothing, and the parcel comes back to us.

Please use this only when you genuinely need to. Repeatedly refusing cash-on-delivery parcels costs us the delivery both ways, and we may stop offering cash on delivery to accounts that do it often, or require payment in advance.

If you have already paid

Orders are paid in cash on delivery, so a cancellation before delivery leaves nothing to refund. If money has changed hands in any other situation, it is refunded under the Return & Refund Policy.

When we may cancel an order

We may cancel your order, and will tell you why, if:

  • the item is out of stock or cannot be supplied;
  • your delivery address is outside our delivery area;
  • we cannot reach you on the phone number given, after repeated attempts by us or the courier;
  • delivery was attempted and failed, or the parcel was refused;
  • the order appears fraudulent, or breaches our Terms of Service;
  • a product was listed at an obviously incorrect price — we will always contact you first.

If we cancel, you owe us nothing and any amount you have paid is returned in full.

Questions

Contact us through the support page. Have your order number ready — it is on the order screen in the app.
