Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective 7 August 2026 · Last updated 13 August 2026

We deliver across Bangladesh through our courier partner. Orders are paid in cash when the parcel arrives.

Where we deliver

We deliver anywhere in Bangladesh that our courier partner RoadRush covers. We do not deliver outside Bangladesh.

When you add an address, choose your division, district and thana so the courier can route the parcel correctly. If we cannot deliver to your area we will contact you and cancel the order at no cost.

Delivery times

Destination Estimated delivery
Inside Dhaka Next day
Outside Dhaka Within 3 days

Both are comfortably inside the limits the Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines, 2021 set for online sellers — five days within the city and ten days elsewhere in the country.

The estimated arrival date on the product page in the app is deliberately cautious, because it is worked out before you have chosen a delivery address. The timelines above are what we actually work to, and your confirmed timeline is the one shown on your order once it is placed.

Orders placed on Fridays and public holidays are processed the next working day. Delivery can take longer during Eid, sales periods and bad weather.

Delivery charges

Destination Charge
Inside Dhaka ৳80
Outside Dhaka (rest of Bangladesh) ৳150

The charge is worked out from your delivery address: addresses in Dhaka district pay ৳80, everywhere else in Bangladesh pays ৳150. Districts in Dhaka division that sit outside the city — Gazipur and Narayanganj among them — are charged the ৳150 rate. There is no separate cash-on-delivery handling fee on top of it. Delivery is itemised on the payment screen before you confirm, so you can see it alongside the price of the items.

Tracking your order

Follow your order under Profile → My Orders in the app. The status updates as the parcel moves — confirmed, despatched, out for delivery, delivered. If you have notifications turned on we will alert you at each step.

When the courier arrives

  • Have the exact cash amount ready where possible — couriers may not carry change.
  • Check the parcel is sealed and undamaged before you pay. If it is visibly damaged you can refuse it at no cost.
  • Someone else at your address can accept and pay on your behalf.

If delivery fails

The courier calls you before delivering and will try again if you are not available. How many attempts they make is set by the courier network handling your parcel. If we cannot reach you, or nobody is available to accept and pay, the parcel is returned to us and the order is cancelled.

Repeatedly refusing cash-on-delivery parcels may mean we stop offering cash on delivery to your account.

Who delivers your order

Deliveries are handled by our logistics partner RoadRush and the courier networks it works with. To deliver your parcel they receive your name, phone number, delivery address and the amount to collect — see the Privacy Policy for detail.

Questions

If your order is late or you need to change a delivery address, get in touch through the support page as early as possible — once a parcel is with the courier we may not be able to change it.
