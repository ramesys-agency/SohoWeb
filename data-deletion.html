Deleting Your Data

Effective 27 August 2026 · Last updated 27 August 2026

You can delete your Soho account, and the personal information attached to it, at any time from inside the app. You do not need to ask us, and there is no waiting period.

Delete your account from the app

  1. Open the Soho app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Edit Profile.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when you are asked to.

Deletion takes effect immediately. You will be signed out, and any session you have open on another device stops working straight away.

If you cannot reach the app

If you have uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or would rather we did it for you, email sohoadminbd@gmail.com from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm your identity, action the request, and reply to confirm — normally within 1 working day, and in every case within 30 days.

You can also reach us on WhatsApp or by phone; the numbers are on the Support page.

What is deleted

Your cart, wishlist, saved addresses that are not attached to an order, product reviews, notifications, saved payment methods and push notification tokens are all permanently deleted.

If you have never placed an order, your account record is deleted outright and nothing about you remains.

What is kept, and why

If you have placed orders, those orders are retained as business and accounting records, because we are required to keep them for five years under the Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act, 2012. Your account record is kept only as a stub that those orders can point to: your email address, name, phone number, password, sign-in identifier, profile photo, age, gender and region are all erased from it.

The retained order records still contain the delivery address and the name, email address and phone number that were given to the courier at the time of that order, because that is part of the record of the transaction. They are no longer connected to a working account and cannot be used to sign in.

If you signed in with Google, Apple or Facebook

Deleting your Soho account as described above removes the link between your Soho account and that sign-in provider, along with everything listed under What is deleted above. Soho never receives or stores your password for those services.

Removing Soho from your Google, Apple or Facebook account settings is a separate thing: it stops that service from being used to sign in again, but it does not delete your Soho account or the data held here. To delete your data, use the app or email us as described above.

Other things worth knowing

  • A parcel already on its way to you will still be delivered. Delete your account after it arrives, or contact support to cancel it first.
  • Your email address becomes free to use again. You can sign up with it later, but the new account starts empty — it will not show your previous orders.
  • Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account or its data once it has been deleted.

Full details

What we collect, how long we keep it and what your rights are is set out in full in our Privacy Policy — deletion is covered in section 6 and retention periods in section 7.
