Return & Refund Policy

Effective 7 August 2026 · Last updated 7 August 2026

If something isn't right, we'll put it right. You have 10 days from delivery to request a return or exchange.

How to request a return

  1. Open the Soho app and go to Profile → My Orders.
  2. Open the delivered order and tap Request a Return.
  3. This opens a WhatsApp chat with our support team, already filled in with your order number and the items. Tell us which items you're returning and why.
  4. We'll confirm your request and arrange collection or tell you where to send the parcel.

Once we approve a return it appears on your order in the app, so you can see its progress. If you'd rather not use WhatsApp, email support@soho-bd.com with your order number.

What can be returned

To be accepted, an item must be:

  • requested within 10 days of delivery;
  • unworn, unwashed and undamaged;
  • in its original packaging with all tags and labels still attached;
  • free of perfume, make-up, deodorant marks or smoke.

What cannot be returned

  • Underwear, lingerie and swimwear, for hygiene reasons.
  • Pierced jewellery.
  • Items made or altered to your measurements.
  • Items marked non-returnable or final sale on the product page.
  • Anything damaged after delivery, or returned without tags and packaging.

These exclusions do not apply if the item is faulty, damaged on arrival, or not what you ordered — see below.

If your item is faulty, damaged or wrong

Contact us within 48 hours of delivery and send photographs of the item and its packaging. We will collect it at our cost and send a replacement or a full refund including delivery charges — your choice.

If you can see the parcel is damaged before you pay, you can refuse it at the door at no cost.

Exchanges

To exchange for a different size or colour, request a return as above and tell us what you'd like instead. Exchanges depend on stock; if your preferred item is unavailable we will refund you instead.

You pay the delivery charge to send the item back; we send the replacement out to you at our cost. If the exchange is because the item was faulty, damaged or not what you ordered, both legs are on us.

Return delivery charges

Reason for return Who pays the return delivery
Faulty, damaged or wrong item sent Soho
Changed your mind, or wrong size ordered You
Exchange You send it back, we deliver the replacement

The original delivery charge is refunded only when the return is our fault or the whole order is cancelled.

Refunds

Once your return reaches us we inspect it within 3 working days and confirm the outcome in the app and by email.

Because orders are paid in cash on delivery, there is no card or wallet to refund to. We pay refunds by bKash, Nagad or bank transfer to the account you tell us, normally within 7 working days of the return being approved.

If a return doesn't meet the conditions above we'll contact you before taking any decision, and send the item back to you at your cost.

Cancelling instead of returning

If your order hasn't been despatched yet you can cancel it outright rather than wait and return it — see the Cancellation Policy.

Your legal rights

This policy sits alongside your rights under the Consumer Rights Protection Act, 2009, the Sale of Goods Act, 1930 and the Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines, 2021. It does not replace or reduce them.

If we have not put things right, you can complain to the Directorate of National Consumer Rights Protection (DNCRP). Under section 60 of the Consumer Rights Protection Act, 2009 a complaint must reach the Directorate within 30 days of the cause arising, so do not wait if you are getting nowhere with us.

Our team is available 10 AM – 8 PM. See the support page for every way to reach us.
