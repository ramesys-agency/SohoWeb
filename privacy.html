Privacy Policy

Effective 7 August 2026 · Last updated 7 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information Soho collects when you use our mobile app or website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can delete it. It covers the Soho app for iOS and Android and this website.

Soho is operated by [[REGISTERED LEGAL ENTITY NAME]] of [[REGISTERED BUSINESS ADDRESS]], Bangladesh ("Soho", "we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal information described here.

1. Information you give us

Creating an account

You cannot browse or order without an account. When you register we collect:

  • Email address — required. We send a one-time verification code to it during signup and password reset. The code is stored in hashed form and expires after five minutes.
  • Full name — required.
  • Password — required for email signup, stored only as a cryptographic hash. We never store or transmit your password in readable form and cannot recover it for you.
  • Phone number — collected when you place an order so the courier can reach you.
  • Profile photo, age, gender and region — all optional. You can use Soho fully without providing them, and you can remove them at any time from Edit Profile.

Signing in with Google or Apple

If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your email address, your name, and an identifier that the provider uses for your account. We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

If you use Sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, Apple gives us a private relay address ending in privaterelay.appleid.com instead of your real email. We use that relay address for all account and order email. Apple supplies your name only on your very first sign-in, so we store it at that point.

Delivery addresses

When you save an address we store the street address, area, thana, district, division, city, postal code and country, along with any delivery note you add. If you pick your address from the map or address search, we also store the coordinates and the Google Place identifier for that location so the courier can find you.

Orders

For each order we record the items, sizes, quantities and prices, any coupon applied, delivery and cash-on-delivery charges, applicable VAT and tax, the delivery address, the order status history, and the name, email address and mobile number to be given to the courier.

Things you create in the app

Your cart, wishlist, saved addresses and product reviews are stored against your account. Product reviews, including your name, are visible to other customers.

2. Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token — if you allow notifications, your device is issued a token that lets us send you order updates. Push is optional; the app works without it.
  • Device and connection information — the type of device and app version, and standard server logs including IP address and timestamps, kept for security and troubleshooting.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Payment information

We never see your card details

Soho currently accepts cash on delivery only. You pay the courier in cash when your parcel arrives. We do not collect, process or store card numbers, mobile wallet credentials or bank details. If we add online payment in future, payment details will be handled by the payment provider and this policy will be updated before that happens.

5. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the following, and only as far as each needs it:

  • RoadRush, our delivery partner, and the courier networks it routes parcels through (including Pathao). They receive your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, the delivery area details, a description of the items and the cash amount to collect.
  • Google — if you sign in with Google, and for the address search and map features.
  • Apple — if you sign in with Apple.
  • Expo, which operates the push notification service that delivers order alerts to your device.
  • Google, whose mail servers deliver our verification and order emails.
  • Microsoft Azure, which hosts our servers. Our database, our uploaded images and our file storage all run on that same server rather than on a separate storage company's service.
  • Government authorities, where we are legally required to disclose information.

Our servers are located in Microsoft Azure's Singapore region, so your information is stored outside Bangladesh. Google, Apple and Expo also process data on servers abroad. We do not transfer national ID numbers, biometric data or any other category the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 treats as sensitive.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Edit Profile → Delete account in the app. You do not need to contact us, and there is no waiting period — the deletion happens immediately. Step-by-step instructions, including what to do if you can no longer reach the app, are on the Deleting Your Data page.

What is deleted

Your cart, wishlist, saved addresses that are not attached to an order, product reviews, notifications, saved payment methods and push notification tokens are all permanently deleted. Any session you have open stops working straight away.

If you have never placed an order, your account record is deleted outright and nothing about you remains.

What is kept, and why

If you have placed orders, those orders are retained as business and accounting records, because we are required to keep them. Your account record is kept only as a stub that those orders can point to: your email address, name, phone number, password, sign-in identifier, profile photo, age, gender and region are all erased from it.

The retained order records still contain the delivery address and the name, email address and phone number that were given to the courier at the time of that order, because that is part of the record of the transaction. They are no longer connected to a working account and cannot be used to sign in.

Other things worth knowing

  • A parcel already on its way to you will still be delivered. Delete your account after it arrives, or contact support to cancel it first.
  • Your email address becomes free to use again. You can sign up with it later, but the new account starts empty — it will not show your previous orders.
  • Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account or its data once it has been deleted.

7. How long we keep things

Record Kept for
Account and profile Until you delete your account
Cart, wishlist, notifications, push tokens Until you delete your account or remove them
Orders and related accounting records Five years, the period business records must be kept under the Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act, 2012
Verification codes Five minutes
Server and security logs Not archived. They stay on the server that produced them and are discarded when the service is restarted or updated

8. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Access to customer data is limited to staff who need it to run the store. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your personal information we will notify you, and we will notify the National Data Management Authority as section 20 of the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 requires.

9. Children

Soho is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights and choices

We process your information because you asked us to supply goods — the contract between us — and, where the law requires it, to keep tax and accounting records. Optional things like push notifications and marketing run on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time without losing anything else. This is the basis section 5 of the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026 requires us to have.

  • See or correct your details — from Edit Profile in the app, or by contacting us. We action correction requests within 30 days.
  • Get a copy of your data — email us and we will provide it in a readable, portable form.
  • Delete your account — from the app, as described in section 6. What we must keep, and why, is set out there.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off notifications in your device settings, or tell us to stop marketing messages.
  • Complain — write to us first. You can also complain to the National Data Management Authority, the regulator under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2026.
