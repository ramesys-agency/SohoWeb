Common questions

Where is my order?

Open Profile → My Orders in the app to see its current status. Estimated delivery times are in the Shipping & Delivery Policy. If your order is past its estimate, message us on WhatsApp with the order number.

How do I return something?

Open the delivered order in the app and tap Request a Return. It opens a WhatsApp chat with your order details filled in. Full conditions are in the Return & Refund Policy.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, free of charge before it's despatched. See the Cancellation Policy.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery only — you pay the courier when your parcel arrives. We don't take card or online payments, and never ask for card details.

I didn't get my verification code

Codes are sent by email and expire after five minutes. Check your spam folder, then request a new one. If it still doesn't arrive, email us from the address you're trying to register.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Edit Profile → Delete account. It takes effect immediately. Full instructions, and what is deleted versus what we have to keep, are on the Deleting Your Data page.

How do I report an inappropriate review?

Email sohoadminbd@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp with the product name and the reviewer's name. See the Terms of Service for what we allow.

Complaints

If we've got something wrong and support hasn't resolved it, write to sohoadminbd@gmail.com marking it as a complaint, or to our registered address. We'll acknowledge within 2 working days and aim to resolve within 15 working days.

If you are still not satisfied, you can take the matter to the Directorate of National Consumer Rights Protection (DNCRP), the regulator for consumer complaints in Bangladesh. Under section 60 of the Consumer Rights Protection Act, 2009 your complaint must reach the Directorate within 30 days of the problem arising.
